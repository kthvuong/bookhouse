Privacy & Lock

A password screen so a stranger with your hosted link can't casually browse your journal. This runs entirely in your browser — it's a deterrent, not bank-grade security.

Forgot it? Open your browser's console on this page and run localStorage.clear(), then refresh — you'll be asked to set a new password. Your library itself lives in IndexedDB and is untouched by this.

Backup & Export

Your reading data lives entirely in this browser. Back it up regularly — it never depends on Open Library staying available.

Restoring replaces everything currently in your library with the contents of the backup file.
